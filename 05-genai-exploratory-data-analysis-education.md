# Generative AI for Exploratory Data Analysis in Education

**Role:** Analyst and solution designer
**Program:** Diplomado en Inteligencia Artificial Generativa — Asturias Corporación Universitaria
**Course:** Aplicaciones de Negocio y Gestión de Riesgos

---

## The challenge

Educational institutions accumulate a great deal of process data and act on very little of it. The
constraint is rarely the data itself. It is that nobody has the hours to explore it, find the redundancies,
and surface where decisions are quietly going wrong.

This project asked whether generative AI could close that gap without introducing new risk, and treated
the second half of that question as seriously as the first.

## The approach

I implemented a functional solution applying generative AI to exploratory data analysis of an educational
process selected for its optimization potential. The work extends the prototype from project 04 into
operational territory: from assisting a teacher to informing an institution.

The analysis had to be traceable. AI-assisted EDA that produces a conclusion nobody can reconstruct is
worse than no analysis, because it carries unearned authority. Advanced prompt design was therefore
built around outputs that are coherent, traceable, and aligned to defined organizational objectives.

## What I delivered

- Analysis of an educational process with identified optimization opportunities, framed organizationally
- AI-assisted exploratory data analysis workflow surfacing patterns, redundancies, and decision-making risks
- Advanced prompt library designed for traceable analytical output
- Evaluation of ethical, legal, and organizational risk

## What this demonstrates

Comfort with the data and business side of learning and development, where instructional design meets
operational decision-making. It also shows risk assessment treated as part of the design work rather than
as a compliance form filled in afterward.
