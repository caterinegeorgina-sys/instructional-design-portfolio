# Generative AI Prototype for Teaching Practice

**Role:** Solution designer and prototyper
**Program:** Diplomado en Inteligencia Artificial Generativa — Asturias Corporación Universitaria
**Course:** Fundamentos y Habilidades Esenciales

---

## The challenge

Three pressures converge on the same person. Student profiles are more diverse than the standard lesson
plan accounts for. Teacher workload has grown past what the working day absorbs. And expectations for
personalized, accessible learning experiences keep rising.

Something has to give, and what usually gives is differentiation, because it is the most time-expensive
thing a teacher does.

## The approach

I designed and prototyped a generative AI solution supporting three specific parts of the workflow:
lesson planning, instructional materials creation, and assessment design.

The design uses ChatGPT, Copilot, and Gemini as pedagogical assistants for drafting, differentiated
activity design, and authentic assessment structuring. The deliberate constraint throughout is that the
tools produce drafts, not decisions. Professional judgment and human validation are retained at every
step, and the workflow is built so that skipping validation is visible rather than silent.

The target was time returned to the teacher without quality traded away, which meant the prototype had to
be evaluated on the quality of what it produced, not on how much faster it ran.

## What I delivered

- Needs analysis identifying which tasks are genuinely delegable and which are not
- Solution architecture across planning, materials, and assessment
- Prompt design for differentiated activities and authentic assessment
- Working prototype with documented quality criteria and validation checkpoints

## What this demonstrates

Practical AI implementation grounded in the realities of instructional work, plus prompt engineering
treated as a design discipline with traceable, evaluable output rather than as trial and error.
