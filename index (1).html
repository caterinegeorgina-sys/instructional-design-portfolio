I design competency-based curriculum and accessible digital learning. For eight years I designed curriculum for SENA, Colombia's national vocational training service, reassigned across five regional campuses and rebuilding the same competency framework for learners with very different prior education and access to technology. Since 2023 I have worked in special education in Utah, with students who have multiple disabilities and deafblindness.

That combination is why accessibility shows up in the first storyboard of my work rather than in a compliance review at the end. When you have watched a student fail to use a resource that passed every visual check, you stop treating WCAG as paperwork.

I hold a Master of Education in Instructional Design and Technology, earned Summa Cum Laude with a perfect 4.00 GPA, and a Máster en Educación y Desarrollo Digital Pedagógico from Instituto Europeo de Posgrado, Madrid.

On language. Each project has an English brief; the full source documents are in Spanish, the language of the graduate programs that produced them. That is deliberate and it is part of what I offer — I design, write, and deliver in both languages, so a course can ship bilingual without a translation vendor in the middle.

On authorship. Every project marked Sole author was designed, written, and produced entirely by me. Collaborative work names my specific contribution and my collaborators' roles.

Featured project

01

Accessible Pictogram Communication System

Sole author

A daily-use pictogram communication system in a special education classroom had never been audited for accessibility, because it looked simple. It failed five of eight WCAG criteria. I rebuilt it as an interactive HTML resource with alternative text on every pictogram, speech synthesis, an AAC message panel, high contrast mode, adjustable text sizing, and full keyboard operability — using only open, free technologies, no installation, and no internet connection required. The full cycle: audit → diagnosis → design rationale → working build → compliance mapping.

Sole author

10

Multisensory Narrative System for Deafblindness and Multiple Disabilities

Base concept developed with Iris Góngora; this deafblindness adaptation is my own

A narrative system delivering stories through all eight sensory systems — including the vestibular, proprioceptive, and interoceptive channels usually left out of "multisensory" resources — with generative AI producing the adapted stories and a human carrying the mediation. Digital resources paired with physical objects, because a story that lives only on a screen is inaccessible to a learner who reads the world by hand.
