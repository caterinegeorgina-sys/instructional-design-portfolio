I design competency-based curriculum and accessible digital learning. For eight years I designed
curriculum for SENA, Colombia's national vocational training service, reassigned across five regional
campuses and rebuilding the same competency framework for learners with very different prior education
and access to technology. Since 2023 I have worked in special education in Utah, with students who have
multiple disabilities and deafblindness.

That combination is why accessibility shows up in the first storyboard of my work rather than in a
compliance review at the end. When you have watched a student fail to use a resource that passed every
visual check, you stop treating WCAG as paperwork.

I hold a Master of Education in Instructional Design and Technology, earned **Summa Cum Laude** with a
perfect 4.00 GPA, and a Máster en Educación y Desarrollo Digital Pedagógico from Instituto Europeo de

## Featured project

### [01 · Accessible Pictogram Communication System](01-accessible-pictogram-communication-system.md)

**Sole author.** A daily-use pictogram communication system in a special education classroom had never
been audited for accessibility, because it looked simple. It failed five of eight WCAG criteria. I rebuilt
it as an interactive HTML resource with alternative text on every pictogram, speech synthesis, an AAC
message panel, high contrast mode, adjustable text sizing, and full keyboard operability — using only
open, free technologies, no installation, and no internet connection required.

**The full cycle: audit → diagnosis → design rationale → working build → compliance mapping.**

## Projects

### [02 · Accessibility Audit of Classroom Learning Materials](02-accessibility-audit-of-classroom-materials.md)

**Sole author.** Structured WCAG and UDL evaluation of a material in daily classroom use. Five of eight
criteria failed. Produced a barrier inventory mapped to affected learner populations and a phased
improvement plan — which became the build in project 01.

### [03 · Generative AI Governance Model for Educational Decision-Making](03-genai-governance-model-inclusive-education.md)

**Sole author.** Strategic model for integrating generative AI into pedagogical, analytical, and
organizational processes in inclusive education, pairing language-model tooling with UDL principles.
Includes governance structure, mandatory human validation checkpoints, ethical and legal risk assessment,
and viability analysis.

### [04 · Generative AI Prototype for Teaching Practice](04-genai-prototype-for-teaching-practice.md)

**Sole author.** Designed and prototyped an AI-assisted workflow for lesson planning, materials creation,
and assessment design. Built so the tools produce drafts, never decisions, with human validation visible
at every step.

### [05 · Generative AI for Exploratory Data Analysis in Education](05-genai-exploratory-data-analysis-education.md)

**Sole author.** Functional implementation applying generative AI to exploratory analysis of an
educational process, surfacing patterns, redundancies, and decision-making risks. Built around traceable
output and a full ethical, legal, and organizational risk evaluation.

### [06 · ICT-Based Methodologies for Inclusive Learning](06-ict-methodologies-for-inclusive-learning.md)

**Sole author.** Analysis of active learning methodologies against what they silently assume a learner can
do, and design of ICT-supported entry points for students with multiple disabilities — the population most
often left out of student-centered approaches.

### [07 · Functional Profile and Support Needs for Transition to Adult Life](07-thesis-proposal-transition-to-adult-life.md)

**Sole author.** Master's thesis case study using the SOCIEVEN functional assessment across nine domains,
triangulating direct observation, caregiver interview, and documentary review to map support needs for a
young adult with multiple disabilities entering adult life. In progress.

### [08 · Prompt Engineering for Special Education: A Controlled Comparison](08-prompt-engineering-comparison-special-education.md)

**Sole author.** Same instructional task, two prompts, both outputs reproduced unedited. The basic version
returns a generic activity and "observe whether the student participates." The expert version returns a
named learning objective, pictogram-based anticipation, stimulus reduction, extended response time, and an
observation protocol. Prompt design treated as an evaluable discipline rather than a knack.

### [09 · AI-Built Multimedia Resource for an Inclusive Classroom](09-ai-multimedia-resource-inclusive-classroom.md)

**Sole author.** Built the classroom resource for the activity generated in project 08: an accessible
presentation and companion video with visual supports carrying the sequence, so the resource does not
depend on verbal comprehension. UDL-led design, AI-generated media held to an accessibility standard,
usable in class and for family reinforcement at home.

### [10 · Multisensory Narrative System for Deafblindness and Multiple Disabilities](10-multisensory-narrative-system-deafblindness.md)

**Base concept developed with Iris Góngora; this deafblindness adaptation is my own.** A narrative
system delivering stories through all eight sensory systems — including the vestibular, proprioceptive,
and interoceptive channels usually left out of "multisensory" resources — with generative AI producing the
adapted stories and a human carrying the mediation. Digital resources paired with physical objects,
because a story that lives only on a screen is inaccessible to a learner who reads the world by hand.

### [11 · Person-Centered Plan: A Transdisciplinary Model](11-person-centered-plan-transdisciplinary.md)

**Special education lead and process coordinator within a six-person transdisciplinary team.** A
person-centered plan for a child with multiple disabilities, coordinating physical therapy,
speech-language pathology, physical education, early childhood education, and the family into one
document rather than six parallel ones. Built on the Rowland Communication Matrix and the ICF functional
framework, with equine therapy integrated for postural and sensory regulation. Published de-identified.
