## Featured project

### [01 · Accessible Pictogram Communication System](01-accessible-pictogram-communication-system.md)

A daily-use pictogram communication system in a special education classroom had never been audited for
accessibility, because it looked simple. It failed five of eight WCAG criteria. I rebuilt it as an
interactive HTML resource with alternative text on every pictogram, speech synthesis, an AAC message
panel, high contrast mode, adjustable text sizing, and full keyboard operability — using only open,
free technologies, no installation, and no internet connection required.

**The full cycle: audit → diagnosis → design rationale → working build → compliance mapping.**

## Projects

### [02 · Accessibility Audit of Classroom Learning Materials](02-accessibility-audit-of-classroom-materials.md)
Structured WCAG and UDL evaluation of a material in daily classroom use. Five of eight criteria failed.
Produced a barrier inventory mapped to affected learner populations and a phased improvement plan — which
became the build in project 01.

### [03 · Generative AI Governance Model for Educational Decision-Making](03-genai-governance-model-inclusive-education.md)
Strategic model for integrating generative AI into pedagogical, analytical, and organizational processes
in inclusive education, pairing language-model tooling with UDL principles. Includes governance structure,
mandatory human validation checkpoints, ethical and legal risk assessment, and viability analysis.

### [04 · Generative AI Prototype for Teaching Practice](04-genai-prototype-for-teaching-practice.md)
Designed and prototyped an AI-assisted workflow for lesson planning, materials creation, and assessment
design. Built so the tools produce drafts, never decisions, with human validation visible at every step.

### [05 · Generative AI for Exploratory Data Analysis in Education](05-genai-exploratory-data-analysis-education.md)
Functional implementation applying generative AI to exploratory analysis of an educational process,
surfacing patterns, redundancies, and decision-making risks. Built around traceable output and a full
ethical, legal, and organizational risk evaluation.

### [06 · ICT-Based Methodologies for Inclusive Learning](06-ict-methodologies-for-inclusive-learning.md)
Analysis of active learning methodologies against what they silently assume a learner can do, and design
of ICT-supported entry points for students with multiple disabilities — the population most often left out
of student-centered approaches.

### [07 · Functional Profile and Support Needs for Transition to Adult Life](07-thesis-proposal-transition-to-adult-life.md)
Master's thesis case study using the SOCIEVEN functional assessment across nine domains, triangulating
direct observation, caregiver interview, and documentary review to map support needs for a young adult
with multiple disabilities entering adult life. In progress.
