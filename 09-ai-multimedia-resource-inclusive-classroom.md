# AI-Built Multimedia Resource for an Inclusive Classroom

**Role:** Instructional designer
**Program:** Diplomado en manejo de herramientas de IA aplicadas a la gestión educativa —
Universidad Politécnica Salesiana del Ecuador
**Focus:** Multiple disabilities · UDL · Accessible multimedia

---

## The challenge

An activity plan is not a resource. The plan says a student will explore contrasting colors and soft
sounds; the student still needs something in front of them — visuals that anticipate what happens next,
a structure that does not depend on following spoken instructions, pacing that a dysregulated learner can
survive.

Building that by hand is the work that gets cut when the day runs out, which is precisely why the
classroom fills up with resources designed for a learner who isn't in the room.

## The approach

I took the activity generated by the expert prompt in project 08 — a structured sensory experience
targeting functional communication, joint attention, and emotional self-regulation — and built the actual
classroom resource for it.

The design is UDL-led rather than decorated after the fact: visual supports carry the sequence so the
resource does not rely on verbal comprehension, the structure anticipates each transition, and the
stimulus load is deliberately low for a learner with sensory sensitivities.

ChatGPT handled the pedagogical design and prompt work. Canva built the presentation and the video,
including AI-generated imagery matched to the activity. Every output was reviewed against the learner
profile before it was kept.

## What I delivered

- Structured prompt specifying learner profile, UDL frame, and required components
- Accessible multimedia presentation with visual supports for each phase of the activity
- Companion video usable for anticipation, instruction, or family reinforcement at home
- Documented rationale for the classroom use of the resource and the ethical basis of the AI workflow

## What this demonstrates

End-to-end delivery: prompt design, pedagogical validation, and a finished accessible artifact — not a
plan describing one. It also shows AI-generated media held to an accessibility standard rather than used
for speed alone, and a resource built to extend past the classroom into the home, where reinforcement
actually happens.
