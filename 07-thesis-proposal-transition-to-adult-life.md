# Functional Profile and Support Needs for Transition to Adult Life

**Role:** Principal researcher · **Type:** Master's thesis, case study design · **Status:** In progress
**Program:** Maestría en Educación Especial, mención Discapacidad Múltiple — Universidad Politécnica
Salesiana, Guayaquil, Ecuador
**Instrument:** SOCIEVEN functional educational assessment

---

## The challenge

Transition to adult life is where support systems for people with multiple disabilities most often fail.
Services are organized around school, and when school ends, the scaffolding ends with it, frequently
without anyone having documented what the person can actually do, what they need, and what would let
them keep participating.

Standardized instruments compound the problem. Designed around normative developmental sequences, they
tend to return a profile of deficits for a young adult with complex support needs, which is precisely the
information least useful for planning a life.

## The approach

A qualitative single-case study characterizing the functional profile and support needs of a young adult
with multiple disabilities and complex support needs, using the SOCIEVEN functional educational assessment
across nine domains.

Findings are triangulated across three sources: direct observation in natural settings, a semi-structured
interview with caregivers or legal guardian, and documentary review. The triangulation is the
methodological core. Direct observation captures what the person does when conditions are favorable; the
caregiver interview captures what happens across contexts an observer never sees; documentary review
supplies the trajectory. Any one alone produces a distorted profile.

Ethical design and confidentiality safeguards are built into the protocol, as required when the
participant cannot independently consent.

## Research question

What functional profile and support needs does a young adult with multiple disabilities present, and what
person-centered recommendations follow for their transition to adult life?

## Objectives

- Describe functional performance across the nine SOCIEVEN domains
- Analyze communication, self-care, mobility, social interaction, and participation in family and
  community routines
- Identify strengths, contextual barriers, and support needs through source triangulation
- Formulate person-centered educational and functional recommendations for supported transition

## What this demonstrates

Research design competence: instrument selection justified against the population, a triangulation
strategy that anticipates the weakness of each source, and a defined analysis approach. The same
discipline that makes a needs analysis trustworthy rather than decorative.
